Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Who we are

Wilhelm Cold Brew is a small-batch cold brew company in St. Louis, Missouri. This policy explains what personal information we collect through wilhelmcoldbrew.com, how we use it, and who we share it with. Questions go to ben@wilhelmcoldbrew.com.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your email address, when you join the Friday Drop list.
  • Your mobile number, if you choose to get drop texts.
  • Your name, shipping address and contact details, when you place an order.
  • Anything you send us by email or text.

Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • The pages you visit, the site or ad that sent you here, and how you use the page (for example, whether you scrolled or started the signup form).
  • Your IP address, approximate location (country, region and city), and browser and device type.
  • Advertising identifiers that arrive in the link you clicked, such as campaign tags and ad click IDs.
  • Which version of a page you were shown, when we are testing two designs.

How we use it

  • To send the emails and texts you signed up for.
  • To take, ship and support your orders, and to send order and tracking updates.
  • To understand how the site and our ads are performing, and to improve them.
  • To prevent spam, fraud and abuse of the signup form and checkout.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and your browser's local storage to keep the site working, to remember which ad or link first brought you here, and to measure visits.

We also use advertising tools from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), X and Reddit. These tools set their own cookies and receive information about your visit, such as the pages you viewed and whether you joined the list. When you join, we send Meta and Reddit a hashed (scrambled) version of your email address, along with technical details like your IP address and browser type, so they can match signups to the ads people saw. Those companies use this information under their own privacy policies.

You can limit this by using your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker, and through the ad settings in your Meta, X and Reddit accounts.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business: payment processing (Stripe), email and text message delivery, website hosting, and shipping carriers and label providers.
  • The advertising platforms described above.
  • Authorities or other parties when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and customers.
  • A buyer or successor, if the business is ever sold or reorganized.

Text messages

Drop texts are optional and are never a condition of purchase. If you opt in, you will get recurring automated messages about drops. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, or HELP for help.

We do not share mobile numbers or text message consent with third parties for their own marketing.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from email with the link at the bottom of any message.
  • Stop texts by replying STOP.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion by emailing ben@wilhelmcoldbrew.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy law. Email us and we will honor any request the law gives you.

How long we keep it

We keep list information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep order records for as long as we need them for tax, accounting and customer support.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
