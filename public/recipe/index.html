How to Brew It

The cut · Over ice · Small batch

Wilhelm ships as a concentrate, so it's meant to be cut before you drink it. Here's the ratio, and why it lands where it does.

Ratio to cut
3:2
Cold brew3 parts
Water2 parts

Why 3:2 and not 1:1

A medium roast usually cuts one-to-one. This one is a light roast, and the flavor works out a little better when it's left a touch stronger on the cold brew side — so we lean it to three parts concentrate, two parts water.

Treat it as a starting line, not a law. Pull it back toward 1:1 if you want it lighter; the barrel does the rest.

The pour

  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Add 3 parts Wilhelm concentrate to 2 parts cold water — or milk, if that's your morning.
  3. Stir once, taste, and adjust to your own liking.

A word on caffeine

About 3 oz of the concentrate carries roughly 150–175 mg of caffeine — a full cup's worth before you cut it. Be mindful of how much you're pouring.
